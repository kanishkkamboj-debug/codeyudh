THE WAR TABLETS (FAQ)

Click any inscribed stone tablet to unveil tactical answers.

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE? +
The hackathon is open to undergraduate engineering students from recognized universities and institutions.
WHAT IS THE TEAM SIZE? +
Each squad must consist of 3 to 4 members. Interdisciplinary squads are warmly encouraged.
WHAT IS THE FORMAT OF THE HACKATHON? +
Code Yudh follows a hybrid format: Phase 1 is an online PPT screening round, followed by Phase 2—an intense offline 24-hour hackathon at Chandigarh University.
WHAT HAPPENS IN PHASE 1? +
Teams submit a presentation describing their problem statement, proposed solution, innovation, and technical approach.
CAN WE SUBMIT A PROTOTYPE OR GITHUB LINK? +
Yes! Teams may optionally include a prototype, video demo, or GitHub repository link with their Phase 1 PPT submission.
HOW ARE PHASE 1 SUBMISSIONS EVALUATED? +
Phase 1 submissions are evaluated on Innovation, Feasibility, Technical Approach, and Potential Impact.
WHAT HAPPENS AFTER PHASE 1? +
Shortlisted teams advance to the offline 24-hour finale at Chandigarh University, Mohali.
WHERE AND WHEN WILL THE FINALE TAKE PLACE? +
The 24-hour offline finale will take place on 27–28 October 2026 at the Chandigarh University campus in Mohali, Punjab.
WHAT WILL TEAMS DO DURING THE FINAL ROUND? +
Teams will build, test, refine, and demonstrate their working prototypes under the mentorship of industry and faculty experts.
HOW WILL FINAL PROJECTS BE EVALUATED? +
Final projects are judged on Innovation, Implementation, Functionality of the live prototype, and Real-world Impact.
